All other figures are **[ESTIMATE]**, produced by this method:

1. Anchor on the sourced head term (49,500).
2. Apply standard modifier-decay ratios observed in commercial B2C niches: `[head] challenge` ≈ 20–28% of head; `how to [verb] [head]` ≈ 5–10%; `best [head] for X` ≈ 3–8%; brand+modifier ≈ 1–5%; long-tail (4+ words) ≈ 0.3–2%.
3. Cross-check against the observed density of ranking content (a keyword with 40 dedicated pages competing for it is not a 50/mo keyword).
4. Round to a band, never a false-precision integer.

**KD (Keyword Difficulty) is scored 0–100 [ESTIMATE]**, weighted heavily by one niche-specific factor: **prop firm SERPs are dominated by affiliate sites earning $50–150 per referral**, which means they can out-invest you in content by an order of magnitude. A KD-25 keyword in a normal niche behaves like KD-45 here. **The scores below already include that penalty.**
