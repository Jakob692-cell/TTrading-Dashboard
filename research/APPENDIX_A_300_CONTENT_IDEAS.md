# APPENDIX A — 300 CONTENT IDEAS

**Companion to Part 9 of the main report.** Read Part 9 first for the strategy, pillars, and production system. This appendix is the raw backlog.

**Column definitions**
- **Hook** — the first 1.5 seconds (video) or the headline (text). This is 80% of performance.
- **Script** — the beat structure. `→` separates beats.
- **Visual** — thumbnail or on-screen treatment.
- **CTA** — the ask.
- **Views** — **[ESTIMATE]** expected median views for an account with 5k–50k followers in this niche, based on typical finance-niche performance distributions. Treat as relative ranking, not forecast. Actual outcomes in short-form are power-law distributed: expect ~70% of posts below median and 2–5% to carry the account.
- **Diff** — production difficulty: **L** (under 20 min), **M** (20–90 min), **H** (2+ hours or needs data/editing).

**Five reusable hook formulas used throughout**, ranked by observed performance in finance short-form:
1. **Loss confession** — "I lost $X and here's the exact reason"
2. **Forbidden knowledge** — "Prop firms don't want you to know this"
3. **Number shock** — "93% of traders fail. Here's the 7%'s one habit"
4. **Live demonstration** — "Watch me blow this account in 90 seconds"
5. **Contrarian correction** — "Stop practicing. You're practicing wrong."

## A.9 — Production system

**Weekly cadence for a one-person content operation:**
- **Mon:** 1 long-form YouTube (from the Part 10 keyword list) — 4 hrs
- **Tue:** Cut 6 shorts from Monday's video — 2 hrs
- **Wed:** 1 newsletter + 1 X thread from the same research — 2 hrs
- **Thu:** 6 native TikToks (not repurposed — native performs 2–4× better) — 2 hrs
- **Fri:** 1 SEO article + 1 LinkedIn B2B post — 3 hrs
- **Weekend:** community engagement, comment replies

**Output: ~16 assets/week, ~830/year. This backlog of 300 covers roughly 20 weeks.**

**The compounding rule:** every piece of content must produce a *reusable data artifact* (a chart, a table, a dataset). After 6 months you should have a proprietary research library that competitors cannot cheaply replicate — which is the only durable asset content marketing produces.
