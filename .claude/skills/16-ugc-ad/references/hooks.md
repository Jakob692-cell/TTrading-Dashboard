# UGC Spoken Hook Patterns

These are things the AVATAR SAYS in the first 1–2 seconds, not visual hooks (see
`07-ecommerce-ad/references/hooks.md` and `11-social-hook/references/hook-craft.md` for
those). A UGC hook must sound like the middle of a real conversation, not an opening line.

## 12 proven spoken hook patterns

1. **Mid-thought interrupt**: "—okay wait, I need to tell you about this."
2. **Direct callout**: "If you have [problem], stop scrolling."
3. **Confession**: "I was today years old when I found out..."
4. **Unpopular opinion**: "Everyone's using [common product] wrong."
5. **Result-first**: "This is what my [skin/desk/hair] looked like before I found this."
6. **Question hook**: "Why did nobody tell me about this sooner?"
7. **Skepticism flip**: "I did NOT think this was going to work."
8. **Relatable complaint**: "I'm so tired of [common frustration]."
9. **Dollar hook**: "I spent $[X] trying to fix this before I found the $[Y] answer."
10. **Time-pressure**: "You have until [event/season] to fix this."
11. **Comparison bait**: "This vs. what I used to use..."
12. **Whisper/secret framing**: "Nobody's talking about this but..."

## Selecting a hook by mode

| Mode | Best-fit hooks |
|---|---|
| UGC (general testimonial) | Confession, Relatable complaint, Result-first |
| Tutorial | Question hook, Unpopular opinion |
| Unboxing | Mid-thought interrupt, Skepticism flip |
| Product Review | Comparison bait, Dollar hook |
| UGC Virtual Try On | Result-first, Whisper/secret framing |

## Delivery notes

- The hook line should be short enough to say naturally in under 2 seconds — read it
  aloud when drafting; if it takes longer, cut it.
- Avoid hook lines that name the brand/product immediately — save the product name for
  the reveal beat. Naming it in the hook reads as an ad, which is what UGC is trying to
  not look like.
- Pair the spoken hook with a matching `hook_id` preset from `show_marketing_studio`
  where the visual mechanic (e.g. "object flies into frame," "jump cut zoom") reinforces
  rather than fights the spoken line.
