How to Patrol

The Job

Radio calls pop up all over the mountain. Follow the red smoke, grab a toboggan from the rack at the summit hut, load the patient and get them to the clinic at the base — fast and gentle.

Every crash on the way hurts your patient. Tip the sled and they go flying.

Earn the daily quota before the lifts close at 16:30 or you're fired.

Avalanches

Orange flags mark avalanche zones. The danger rises all day. Toss charges (crate at the hut) onto a cornice to trigger a controlled slide for cash.

If someone gets buried, your beacon turns on. Follow the beeps, then everyone hold E to dig — together it's faster. Air runs out!
