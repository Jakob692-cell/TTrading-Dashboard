How to Patrol

Der Einsatz · The call

The pager goes off and Leitstelle Talheim sends a mission. Press X on the radio to take it („übernehme“). Follow the red smoke.

At the scene, plant crossed skis 10 m uphill (E), or the next skier may crash into you (Folgeunfall).

First aid: pick each measure in the right order with 1–4. Watch out for myths. Never pop a shoulder back in!

Then load the patient into the akja and bring them down to the Unfallambulanz. A teammate can take the rear rope (E) to keep the akja steady.

Lawine · Avalanche

The Lawinenlagebericht (1–5) at the station sets the risk. Throw charges (G) onto cornices for controlled releases.

Buried? LVS search: signal, then coarse, then fine search. At under 3 m, probe (E) until you hit, then dig together. After ~1 minute the survival curve drops fast.

Head, spine or hip injuries need the helicopter „Föhn 1“: request it with X, stand on flat open ground and hold E to marshal it in (arms up!). No flights in storms or at dusk.

At closing time comes the last sweep: send the stragglers off the slopes.
